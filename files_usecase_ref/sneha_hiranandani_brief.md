### AI Use Cases in Motion

From **Cipla Annual Report FY 2023-24** and corroborated by MatrixBCG analysis:

- Cipla committed to **15 AI use cases** across business functions in FY 2023-24
- Plans were announced to significantly expand in FY 2024-25

From the **Annual Report FY 2024-25** (Cipla-AR-2024-25.pdf, page 3):

> _"We remain committed to advancing our innovation capabilities by harnessing data science and digital technology in our R&D efforts."_

Also from the FY 2024-25 Annual Report:

> _"We launched CipAir, an AI-powered mobile application, to simplify asthma screening in India with state-of-the-art proprietary technology that uses breathing signatures for Asthma screening."_

**CipAir launch** (January 6, 2025 — cipla.com press release):
Cipla launched CipAir, an AI-powered mobile app for first-line asthma screening, integrated into their Breathefree respiratory care platform. The app uses acoustic analysis and artificial neural networks to screen for asthma. This is a live, shipped AI product — not a pilot. It was described as "part of the company's innovation agenda aimed to empower Indians with tech-powered health solutions."

This is the most concrete evidence of active, production AI deployment Sneha's team is responsible for.
